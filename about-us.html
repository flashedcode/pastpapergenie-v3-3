About

Hi, I'm xrompf (Ryan), the owner and maintainer of pastpapergenie and xrompf-dots.

This project started in 2025, just for myself, because I wanted to play Minecraft and other games at school when I was bored.

Since then it has gotten much bigger. Now you can play hundreds of actual good games like Terraria, Minecraft 1.26 and even Call of Duty. There are also 26,000+ anime, a website unblocker and customisable themes.

Thank you!
